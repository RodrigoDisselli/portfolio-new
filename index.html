About me

I’m a UX designer who turns complex systems into clear, confident products.

I work where data-heavy tools meet real people: enterprise platforms, analysis workflows and the small details that make them feel effortless. I lead discovery, map flows, permissions and edge cases, and write requirements and acceptance criteria that engineering and QA can build and test against.

AI is part of how I work every day: it speeds up discovery, keeps research and decisions organized, sharpens team communication, and turns ideas into prototypes faster. I also use it to generate help documentation, build design systems and create interactive AI chat experiences.
